# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Ledgerly is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
level of technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

Community members should:

- communicate respectfully and assume good intent;
- give specific, constructive feedback about ideas and code;
- accept responsibility, apologize, and correct mistakes;
- respect privacy, consent, and differing viewpoints; and
- prioritize the health and safety of the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized
language or attention, trolling, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption,
doxing, publishing private information, or other behavior that a reasonable
person would consider hostile or unsafe.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to Issues, Discussions, pull requests, reviews,
commits, and other Ledgerly community spaces. It also applies when someone
publicly represents the project or its community.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or
permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this policy. Enforcement
decisions will consider context, severity, repetition, impact, and the safety of
affected community members.

Examples of proportionate responses include:

1. A private or public correction for inappropriate language or tone.
2. A formal warning with clear expectations for future participation.
3. A temporary restriction for sustained or serious violations.
4. A permanent ban for harassment, threats, doxing, or repeated violations.

## Reporting harmful content

Use GitHub's **Report content** option when it is available, or submit an abuse
report through [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
Do not publish sensitive personal information in an Issue or Discussion.
Maintainers may moderate visible repository content independently of GitHub's
platform-level review.

Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation. False or malicious reports
may themselves violate this policy.
